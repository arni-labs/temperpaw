# Compare world candidates before freezing

Fresh runs use proposal contract 2. Existing contract 1 runs retain their original behavior.

The food acceptance run admitted four familiar arrangements from five broad Jev labels. Its strongest statement about local protein production disappeared from its load-bearing commitments; topic differences also passed as different worlds. Increasing the same classification confidence would not repair either defect.

The existing SemanticRun imagine/explore/proposals lifecycle now generates 8–12 compact candidates before freezing any endpoint. Imagination sees the question, not a research agenda. An explore turn finds each candidate's closest present analogue, citing typed present findings or explicitly recording an unknown. It records defining commitment IDs and dependent consequence relationships. It may revise a candidate only with a preserved original, replacement and reason. Research uses the existing source ingestion and baseline reconciliation contract.

Jev separately checks narrative-to-commitment coverage, a change relative to the supported present analogue, and each consequence's dependence on the defining change. Unknown analogues cannot qualify as novelty. All viable candidates are compared in pairs when their actual native packed request cost leaves the downstream reserve. No first-six pruning hides later candidates. A deterministic exhaustive subset search selects 3–5 pairwise distinct arrangements; overlap is permitted, complementary topics alone do not qualify. The proposer supplies an explicit fallible comparison priority used for stable selection ties, not probability ranking.

Selected candidates receive one substantive development turn before freezing. This turn may change their statements and commitments to pursue interacting consequences and different arrangements; it is not limited to longer prose. Provisional and developed versions remain recorded. Copied contrast bindings are discarded. The developed set passes through present comparison, coverage, dependence and pair distinction again. Identical requests reuse their exact recorded Jev receipts, while changed claims or context force new checks. Development does not reset the existing research repair budget or repeat indefinitely. Only this accepted set becomes immutable endpoint_search originals. Subsequent route search and explicit evaluated amendments keep their existing contract.

The pool, exact failed relation requests, results, revisions, pair admission and selection receipts remain in program_json. Candidate judgments are not forecast odds. All global limits remain unchanged. Analogue repair permits at most three research attempts and remains subject to native remaining-time/call/transition admission. The proposal planner reserves three initial route turns plus composition/writing; selecting more worlds requires a larger reserve. It measures actual pair packing before admission. This is bounded search, not a guarantee that every alternative route or worst-case provider retry will fit.

Regression fixtures preserve the captured food proposals and exercise missing implication repair, unsupported analogues despite positive labels, complementary slices, later distinct candidates, unchanged-request reuse, changed-baseline invalidation, explicit revisions and same-response source identity mapping. Deterministic Jev response fixtures verify control behavior, not the provider's semantic accuracy. Live output quality still requires deployed observation.

## Stronger present comparisons

The fifth games test accepted reusable game-component marketplaces after comparing them with Roblox avatar goods. That chosen comparison omitted stronger present examples. Jev saw only evidence selected for the analogue, so a positive label could not establish novelty.

New pools require a bounded per-commitment present comparison and a reported research basis. The existing contrast turn searches for stronger current mechanisms rather than relying on its initial analogue. Jev sees all active source findings, including uncited counterexamples; source reports retain their qualifications, and future projections cannot serve as observed present matches. Already-present defining claims or unresolved comparisons cannot authorize a novelty claim merely because another check is positive. Missing evidence remains unresolved, not impossible.

Reported search queries are explicitly researcher reports, not independently verified tool receipts. The engine validates references, chronology and bounds; it cannot certify that a researcher found every relevant example. The actual deployed output and blind comparison remain the quality test. Older saved pools without the comparison contract remain readable.
