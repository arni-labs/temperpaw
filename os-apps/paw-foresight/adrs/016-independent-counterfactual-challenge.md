# Independent counterfactual challenge before composition

The live exploration produced 44 hypotheses, then declared the space sufficiently explored. Most candidates preserved present-day review, approval and cleanup workflows despite instructions to challenge those assumptions. Selecting different combinations cannot recover possibilities absent from the candidate set.

Before accepting the first model-declared saturation, the native engine now runs one `challenge` phase. It supplies the user's question, horizon, conditions and observed evidence, excluding previous candidate prose and scores. The generator identifies changeable causal premises and proposes alternative mechanisms. It cannot add unresearched evidence. Its evidence aliases use a separate namespace; hidden candidate references are rejected rather than silently resolved against the full catalog.

New hypotheses enter the existing expansion and Jev evaluation pipeline. Existing candidates and evaluations remain available for subsequent ordinary exploration and composition. A program receipt records challenged premises and the new hypothesis IDs. Completion means this generation pass finished, not that its claims are accurate or that novelty is guaranteed. The same pass cannot repeat after its receipt is completed. Existing resource and provider limits take precedence.

Verification changes earlier candidate prose and order while requiring identical challenge input; changed observed evidence must change the input. Tests also cover reference isolation, new events receiving fresh evaluation tasks, preserved candidate history and the bounded transition. Disabling the transition makes the regression fail. Live output quality remains an empirical check, not something these structural tests establish.
