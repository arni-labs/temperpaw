# ARN-456 — Ask inbox and review doors

Rita needs one inbox on a long Effort: what she must decide, what she must do, and what the agent already decided. She talks to the implementer; the implementer reads and writes Temper.

Review must refuse on fix-it failures (unused code, overengineering, drift from intent) and send that back to the implementer. Risk facts (auth/secrets, core path, hard to reverse, fundamental change) do not fail review; they decide auto-merge vs a do Ask that carries the PR URL.

After three panel rounds that still fail, the assess review spiral writes a stall Ask. Answering that Ask is Resume. Adjudication is removed.

Reviewers stay one-shot CLIs. The implementer records one ReviewRun per agent in Temper.
